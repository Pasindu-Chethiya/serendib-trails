Privacy & tracking.

How this academic demonstration uses optional tracking.

About this site

Serendib Trails is a student-developed demonstration website. Package descriptions are examples; no reservations are accepted.

Analytics

If you opt into analytics cookies, Google Tag Manager may load Google Analytics 4 tags to collect aggregate website interaction events, such as page views, destination clicks, and demo enquiry completions. These events do not contain your typed name, email, or message.

Marketing

With separate marketing consent, a Meta Pixel tag configured through Google Tag Manager may collect events to measure marketing interactions and support audience creation. Marketing tracking must be configured and published by the project team before it works.

Your choices

Optional tracking is off until you choose your cookie settings. You may reject or change choices using the cookie preferences link in the footer. Consent preferences are stored locally on your browser.

Contact forms

The demonstration enquiry form validates locally only. Its entered fields are not sent to a backend, stored or included in analytics events.

External resources

Photographs load from Unsplash's image servers. This site is a demonstration and should be reviewed for privacy and legal requirements before production use.
